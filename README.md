137 open browser tabs. An honest, lived-in count, not a round guess:
nobody who is guessing says 137. Surprising without being absurd — far
above the tidy dozen most people admit to, yet entirely believable for a
real person right now (a researcher three rabbit holes deep, a bounty
hunter with a tab per lead, a reader who never closes anything "just in
case"). The odd, specific number is what makes it read as a true
confession rather than a number picked to shock, and it hints at a whole
life behind it: the kind of person whose browser is a second memory.
